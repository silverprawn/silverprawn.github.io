Has Greater Manchester kept pace since devolution?

Devolution in England transfers powers and funding from the UK government to regional strategic authorities, including mayor-led bodies responsible for areas such as transport, skills and housing. Greater Manchester signed England’s first devolution deal in November 2014. These six charts compare Greater Manchester (red) with the other strategic authorities shown (grey), with a dashed line marking 2014.

Data: Growth Lab strategic-authority indicators (from ONS, DESNZ), downloaded with a Python loop: Google Colab notebook.
